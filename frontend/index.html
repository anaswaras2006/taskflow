Good to know

A few common questions

Do I need to create an account?

Yes — a quick sign-up (just a username and password) keeps your tasks private to you.

Where is my data stored?

Securely in a database, tied to your account — so it's there whenever you log back in, on any device.

Can I use folders for different projects?

Yes — create as many as you like from the sidebar.

Will I lose my tasks if I clear browser data?

No — your tasks live in your account, not your browser. Just log back in and they'll be there.
